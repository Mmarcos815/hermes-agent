# Course Outline — 20 Modules

1. **Intro to Red Teaming** — Red team vs. pentest vs. bug bounty, adversary lifecycle, legal/ethical frameworks
2. **Recon & OSINT** — Passive vs. active recon, OSINT tools, DNS enumeration, target profiling
3. **Web Application Attacks** — OWASP Top 10, SQLi, XSS, CSRF, SSRF, Burp Suite
4. **Network Scanning** — Nmap techniques, service enumeration, SMB/SNMP/LDAP
5. **Vulnerability Assessment** — Nessus/OpenVAS, CVSS, false positive identification
6. **Exploitation Fundamentals** — Metasploit, manual exploitation, exploit databases
7. **Password Attacks** — Hashcat, John, credential spraying, Kerberoasting
8. **Windows Post-Exploitation** — Priv esc, lateral movement (PsExec/WMI), persistence
9. **Linux Post-Exploitation** — SUID/cron/kernel exploits, lateral movement, exfiltration
10. **Active Directory Attacks** — BloodHound, Golden/Silver Ticket, DCSync
11. **Phishing & Social Engineering** — Campaign creation, payload delivery, pretexting
12. **Evasion & Anti-Forensics** — AV/EDR evasion, LOLBins, log clearing
13. **Command & Control** — C2 architecture, Cobalt Strike/Sliver/Mythic
14. **Web Shells & App Layer** — Web shell deployment, API abuse, cloud exploitation
15. **Wireless & Physical Security** — WPA2/WPA3 attacks, RFID cloning, physical assessments
16. **Cloud & Container Security** — AWS/Azure attack surfaces, container escape, IAM misconfigs
17. **Mobile Device Security** — Android/iOS attack surfaces, app reverse engineering
18. **Reporting & Purple Teaming** — Report writing, remediation, purple team exercises
19. **Threat Intelligence & Adversary Emulation** — MITRE ATT&CK mapping, APT emulation
20. **Capstone Exercise** — Full adversary simulation, peer review, final presentation
