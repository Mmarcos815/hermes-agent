## Training Data Summary

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Total Examples** | 4,750 |
| **Domains Covered** | 33 |
| **Data Type** | Instruction-response pairs, domain-specific Q&A, structured reasoning traces |
| **Languages** | Primarily English |

Training data was curated across 33 distinct domains to ensure broad coverage while maintaining depth in each area. The dataset emphasizes high-quality, human-verified examples with balanced representation across domains.

---

## Training Pipeline (5 Stages)

### Stage 1: Data Curation & Preprocessing
- Aggregated raw data from 33 domains
- Applied deduplication, quality filtering, and format standardization
- Split into train/validation/test sets (80/10/10)

### Stage 2: Base Model Selection
- Evaluated candidate base models on domain-representative benchmarks
- Selected model based on reasoning capability, instruction-following, and efficiency

### Stage 3: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)
- Full-parameter fine-tuning on curated instruction-response pairs
- Learning rate scheduling with warmup and cosine decay
- Gradient accumulation for stable training at target batch size

### Stage 4: Alignment & Safety Training
- Applied Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) with curated preference pairs
- Safety-focused fine-tuning to reduce harmful outputs
- Constitutional AI techniques for value alignment

### Stage 5: Evaluation & Iteration
- Benchmarked against held-out test sets across all 33 domains
- Human evaluation on a representative sample
- Iterative refinement based on failure analysis

## Intended Use

### Primary Use Cases
- Domain-specific question answering across technical and general knowledge areas
- Instruction following for structured task completion
- Reasoning and analytical problem-solving
- Code generation and technical documentation

### Target Users
- Researchers and practitioners requiring domain-adapted language models
- Developers building applications on fine-tuned model backbones
- Educators and students in technical disciplines

### Out of Scope
- Deployment in safety-critical systems without additional safeguards
- Real-time decision-making in high-stakes environments (medical, legal, financial) without human oversight
- Applications requiring guaranteed factual accuracy

## Limitations

1. **Factual Hallucination:** The model may generate plausible-sounding but incorrect information, particularly in specialized domains not well-represented in training data.

2. **Domain Imbalance:** While 33 domains are covered, some domains may have deeper coverage than others, leading to variable performance.

3. **Temporal Cutoff:** Training data has a fixed knowledge cutoff; the model lacks awareness of events after its training period.

4. **Bias:** Despite alignment efforts, residual biases present in the base model and training data may persist.

5. **Language:** Performance is optimized for English; multilingual capabilities are limited.

6. **Reasoning Depth:** Complex multi-step reasoning tasks may produce errors, especially those requiring precise logical deduction or mathematical computation.

7. **Instruction Following Edge Cases:** Unusual or adversarial prompt formats may produce unexpected outputs.

## Ethical Considerations

### Responsible Use
- Users should verify critical information generated by the model against authoritative sources
- The model should not be used to generate deceptive content, misinformation, or material intended to harm
- Outputs should be reviewed before deployment in any user-facing application

### Privacy
- The model was not trained on personal data; however, users should avoid inputting sensitive personal information
- No mechanism exists for the model to recall or reproduce specific training examples verbatim

### Fairness
- Alignment training aims to reduce harmful stereotypes and discriminatory outputs
- Users are encouraged to audit model behavior for their specific demographic and cultural context

### Transparency
- This model card documents known limitations and intended use cases
- Users should disclose AI-generated content where appropriate
