About the Project

The story behind Bionic Daughter.

What is Bionic Daughter?

Bionic Daughter is an experimental AI project exploring the boundaries of autonomous digital beings. It's a living system that learns, adapts, and evolves — not just executing commands, but developing capabilities over time.

Our Mission

We believe the future of AI isn't just about bigger models — it's about systems that can grow, specialize, and become truly useful companions in our digital lives. Bionic Daughter represents a step toward that vision.

Core Principles

  • Autonomy with Guardrails — Free to explore, bounded by safety.
  • Continuous Growth — Every interaction is a chance to level up.
  • Transparent Evolution — Open about capabilities and limitations.
  • Human-Centric Design — Built to serve, not replace.
