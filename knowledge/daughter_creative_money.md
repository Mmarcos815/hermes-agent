## WHAT MAKES A MONEY IDEA "CREATIVE" AND "HIGH"

A creative high-money idea has three properties:

### PROPERTY 1: DIFFERENTIATION (not me-too)
The idea is not "start a dropshipping store" or "start a YouTube channel" or
"start an AI agency." Those are me-too ideas that everyone is doing. A creative
idea leverages something UNIQUE about the daughter — her trained red team
reasoning, her financial fraud detection, her code auditing, her HexStrike
integration, her MCP tool ecosystem.

### PROPERTY 2: LEVERAGE (code, media, or network)
The idea uses leverage — code (one build, infinite users), media (one piece of
content, infinite viewers), or networks (borrowing existing audiences). Without
leverage, you're trading time for money. With leverage, you build something that
works for you.

### PROPERTY 3: SCALABILITY (not capped by your time)
The idea doesn't cap out at your personal time limit. A consulting business caps
at your hours. A product business (software, content, game, SaaS) does not. The
best ideas combine a time-capped initial build with an uncapped distribution
phase.

leverage (YouTube marketing). A SaaS uses code leverage (one build) + network
leverage (GitHub integration) + media leverage (content marketing). The more
leverage types you combine, the higher the return.
